Small Acts. Bigger Ripples. 🌊

RIPPLE Initiative is a youth-led social impact movement dedicated to bringing hope, kindness, and meaningful support to children and elderly people, starting in our school community in Nigeria and growing towards global impact.

Join the Ripple

About RIPPLE

We believe that positive change does not always begin with something huge. Sometimes, it begins with one person choosing to care. RIPPLE gives young people opportunities to turn kindness into action and create meaningful change in their communities.

Our Impact

💗 Children

Supporting children through kindness, resources and community action.

👵 Elderly

Bringing companionship, care and practical support to elderly people.

🤝 Community

Encouraging young people to become positive change-makers.
