About RIPPLE

Change does not always begin with something huge. Sometimes, it begins with one person choosing to care.

RIPPLE Initiative is a youth-led initiative originating in Nigeria with a vision of encouraging compassion, service, and meaningful community action. We aim to create opportunities for young people to support children, show care for elderly people, and contribute positively to their communities.

Our journey begins with small, achievable actions and a commitment to learning, responsibility, and lasting positive change.

Our Mission & Vision

💗

Our Mission

To inspire young people to turn compassion into action through community service, kindness, and support for children and elderly people.

🌍

Our Vision

To grow a connected community of young changemakers whose small acts of kindness create a wider culture of care, dignity, and inclusion.

✨

Our Values

Compassion, dignity, integrity, inclusion, youth leadership, and responsible service guide the work we hope to do.

Meet the Founder

Every ripple begins somewhere. Ours begins with a belief that young people can help make their communities better.

🌸

A Young Nigerian Changemaker

RIPPLE Initiative was founded by a Nigerian secondary-school student who believes that kindness should become action. The initiative was created to explore practical ways young people can contribute to the wellbeing of children, elderly people, and the wider community.

As RIPPLE develops, its founder is committed to learning, building responsible partnerships, documenting real progress, and growing the initiative one meaningful step at a time.
